What I build

Websites for local business

Restaurants, cafes and shops get a site that shows what they offer and how to reach them, clearly, on any screen.

Interface and visual design

Type, color and layout chosen for each brand, then built by hand in HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

AI and web apps

How a project runs

Four steps, in this order.

01

Brief

We talk through what the business does, who visits the site and what they should do there.

02

Design

I lay out the pages and choose type and color for your brand. You see it before it is built.

03

Build

I code it by hand so it loads fast and works well on phones as well as computers.

04

Launch

The site goes live on the web and the people you want to reach can open it.
